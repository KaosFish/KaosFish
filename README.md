## 🌙 Now on the App Store: Mizan

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/icons/mizan.png" width="72" alt="Mizan icon"/>
</p>

<h3 align="center">A prayer companion that asks nothing of you.</h3>
<p align="center"><b>Free · No account · Built for you</b></p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id6771984583"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Download%20on%20the%20App%20Store-0D96F6?style=for-the-badge&logo=appstore&logoColor=white"/></a>
  <a href="https://shinka.ca/mizan/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/shinka.ca%2Fmizan-D4AF64?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=black"/></a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/mizan/phone.svg" width="280" alt="Mizan screens: Today, Qur'an, Qibla, Tasbih, Library"/>
</p>

My first app live on the App Store, built from the ground up and released in September 2026.

- 🕌 Shows the prayer you are **in right now**, not just the next one, with every prayer's window and the karahat times
- 🧮 **22 calculation methods** plus custom angles, both Asr madhhabs and high-latitude rules, with your region's authority picked automatically
- 📖 Full **Qur'an reader** with recitation audio, a **Qibla** compass, **Tasbih**, a **Prayer Log** and **Prayer Lock**
- 🔒 Everything stays on the device: no account, no ads, no analytics, no backend
- 🇬🇧 🇹🇷 English and Türkçe · iPhone + Apple Watch · **Android version in testing on Google Play**
